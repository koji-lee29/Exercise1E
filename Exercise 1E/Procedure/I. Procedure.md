#procedure


**

The material utilized for this experiment consists of Ishiharea color plates, a timer or stopwatch, a data recording sheet, and the corresponding interpretation table provided in the Ishihara color plate book. Prior to the procedure, the interpretation table was prepared, and a data sheet was organized to document the participant’s responses. The participants were instructed to examine each color plate under adequate illumination, preferably bright light or natural sunlight, while maintaining distance of approximately 30 inches and positioning the plates perpendicular to the line of vision. Each plate was presented individually, and the participant was given a maximum of three seconds to identify the numbers or patterns displayed. The laboratory partner recorded each response and subsequently compared the recorded observations with the correct interpretations provided in the Ishihara color plate book. The results were then assessed to determine the presence of possible color vision deficiency and identify its corresponding type, if applicable.
